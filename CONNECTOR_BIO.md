# Claude Connector Directory Bio

The copy below is the canonical listing description for the Agency AI connector in Claude's
connector directory. Keep this file in sync with what is submitted to Anthropic.

---

**Agency AI turns Claude into a CMO for your Meta and Google ads.** Connect once. Claude analyzes, launches, optimizes and reports on your campaigns, on a schedule you set.

**Give it a goal, not a to-do list**

* Keep my blended ROAS above 3 and scale what's working
* Cut wasted spend every morning and tell me what you changed
* Refresh creative on my top campaigns every two weeks

**What Claude sees**
Live campaign performance (spend, revenue, ROAS, conversions), daily trends, budget pacing, spend and ROAS anomalies, and your brand and margin profile. Decisions grounded in your economics, not generic best practice.

**What Claude does**

* Creates and edits ads
* Launches campaigns
* Pauses underperformers
* Shifts budget across campaigns and platforms
* Pulls reports

**Runs on autopilot**
Use Claude's scheduled tasks for a daily budget review, Monday performance report or weekly creative rotation. Every change is shown for your approval before it runs.

**Included:** Analytics Dashboard with Meta, Google and blended ROAS, spend, revenue and cost per result.

Works with any e-commerce platform. Connect Shopify for store revenue and brand context. Faster responses with less token spend than direct-to-ad-platform MCPs.
