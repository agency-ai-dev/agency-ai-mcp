# Agency AI MCP Server

Meta + Google ads analytics, strategy, and execution for Shopify stores, in Claude and ChatGPT.

Agency AI MCP connects your Meta and Google ad accounts for Shopify stores to Claude and ChatGPT. Manage your ad operation conversationally: performance analysis, budget optimization, creative recommendations, campaign strategy, and execution. Faster responses with less token spend than direct-to-ad-platform MCPs.

## What you can do

**Analyst:** campaign data, performance breakdowns, NCROAS and advanced KPIs across Meta and Google.

**Strategist:** budget guidance, creative recommendations, scaling plans, cross-channel campaign planning.

**Execution:** launch, pause, and manage ads across your accounts.

## Requirements

- An Agency AI account (https://agencyai.app)
- - Claude Max, or a ChatGPT plan that supports custom connectors
 
  - ## Connect
 
  - **Claude:** Settings > Connectors > Add custom connector > paste the endpoint URL > authenticate with your Agency AI account.
 
  - **ChatGPT:** Settings > Connectors > Add > paste the endpoint URL > authenticate with your Agency AI account.
 
  - Endpoint: `https://s.agencyai.app/mcp`
 
  - ## Example prompts
 
  - - "How did my Meta campaigns perform last 30 days vs prior 30?"
    - - "Which ads should I iterate on and which should I cut?"
      - - "Where should I shift budget this week?"
